01 / The idea

A 360° view needs
a spherical answer.

Planar boxes are useful detector outputs, yet they cannot fully describe the visible angular support of a person on a sphere. PanoPed pairs a synthetic benchmark with consented real scenes and evaluates tracking in native spherical regions.

03 / The method

A small readout for
the whole sphere.

Sextant reuses the detector's frozen visual query. It predicts a local angular correction while the tracker's identities, scores, detections and history remain untouched.

01

Read

256D detector query, initial geometry and confidence.

02

Correct

Lightweight 262→128→4 head, trained on native support targets.

03

Preserve

Final localization changes; scores, identities and tracker state do not.
